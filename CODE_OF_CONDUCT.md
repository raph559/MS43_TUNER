# Code of conduct

MS43 Tuner adopts the [Contributor Covenant, version 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/) as its code of conduct. The full text at that link applies to everyone who takes part: in issues, pull requests, reviews, discussions and anywhere else the project is represented.

## In short

We want this to be a place where anyone can ask a question, report a problem or send a change and be treated with respect, whatever their experience, background or identity, and whatever car they drive.

That means:

- Be patient and kind, especially with people new to tuning or to open source.
- Disagree about code and data, not about people. Give reasons and evidence.
- Accept a correction gracefully, and own a mistake when you make one.
- No harassment, insults, personal attacks, or sexual language or imagery.
- Do not publish anyone's private information, including their bins, logs or anything that identifies them or their car, without their permission.

## Reporting

If someone's behaviour worries you, report it privately to the maintainer, [@raph559](https://github.com/raph559) on GitHub. Do not report it in a public issue.

GitHub has no direct messages, so the private channel is this repository's private reporting form: open the **Security** tab, choose **Report a vulnerability**, and start the title with "Conduct report". Only the maintainer and you can read it. Say what happened, where (links help) and when.

Every report is read and kept confidential. The maintainer decides what to do as the Contributor Covenant's enforcement guidelines describe, from a private word to a ban from the project. If a report concerns the maintainer, you can also use GitHub's own **Report content** option on the comment or issue.
