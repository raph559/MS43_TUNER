## Third-party parts

### MS4x Dev Team logging definitions

These four TunerPro ADX logging definitions are by the **MS4x Dev Team** (each file names them as its author):

| File | What it is |
|---|---|
| `definitions/Siemens_MS43_Extended_Log_v2.9_M54B22.adx` | Extended Log v2.9, M54B22 |
| `definitions/Siemens_MS43_Extended_Log_v2.9_M54B25.adx` | Extended Log v2.9, M54B25 |
| `definitions/Siemens_MS43_Extended_Log_v2.9_M54B30.adx` | Extended Log v2.9, M54B30 |
| `definitions/Siemens_MS43_MS43X001_Logging.adx` | MS43X001 logging definition, v1.0.0 |

- They are bundled in this repository and embedded in `MS43Logger.Core` when it is built, so they are also inside the published app and CLI.
- They have no stated license. They are **not** covered by this project's GPL license, and nothing in this repository grants any right to them.
- They have not been changed in this repository since they were added.
- They come from the MS4X project: [Logger Definition Files](https://www.ms4x.net/index.php?title=Logger_Definition_Files).
- The maintainer is asking the MS4X project for permission to bundle them. If the MS4x Dev Team asks, they will be removed. To ask for that, open an issue or contact [@raph559](https://github.com/raph559) on GitHub.

### Not included

- **The MS43X001 map definition (XDF)** is not in this repository. It has no stated license. Get it from the MS4X project and open it in the app. `.gitignore` keeps `*.xdf` files out of the repository.
- **Bins** (ECU flash images) are never committed, neither stock nor tuned. `.gitignore` keeps `*.bin` and `*.bak` files out of the repository.
